UltimateMail is a free-software mail client for any IMAP/SMTP account, inspired by iOS Mail and the Gmail app. It works offline first: what you see comes from a local database, and your changes are synced later by a queue that survives restarts.

IMPORTANT: UltimateMail is in early development. It is not ready to replace your daily mail app yet. Reading messages, composing, search, swipe gestures and settings are still being built.

What works today:
* Adding an account with a password or app password, with automatic server settings and a connection test.
* Folders and labels, a conversation list with a unified inbox, and background sync.
* Credentials encrypted with the Android Keystore.

Planned:
* Reading conversations with safe HTML (remote content blocked by default).
* Composing, drafts and an offline send queue.
* Configurable swipe gestures with undo, and a move/label picker with live search.
* Local and server search, per-account signatures, settings.
* Sign-in with Google (own OAuth client) and Microsoft.

Principles:
* No telemetry, no analytics, no ads, no Google services. Your mail only travels between your device and your mail servers.
* Free software under the GPL-3.0-or-later license.
* English and Spanish.
