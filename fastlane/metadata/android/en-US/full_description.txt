UltimateMail is a free-software mail client for any IMAP/SMTP account, inspired by iOS Mail and the Gmail app. It works offline first: what you see comes from a local database, and your changes are synced later by a queue that survives restarts.

First version (0.1). It reads, writes, searches and syncs mail and has been used with Gmail. A review with TalkBack has not been done yet.

What it does:
* Accounts: automatic server settings and connection test; password or app password (Gmail needs an app password); export and import accounts as an encrypted file (no mail inside).
* Reading: conversations with safe HTML (remote content blocked by default, link destinations confirmed), attachments on demand, bodies kept for offline reading.
* Writing: reply, reply all, forward with attachments, drafts, a send queue with undo that works offline, one signature per account.
* Organising: swipe gestures you configure, quick selection, undo, a move and label picker with live search, Gmail labels.
* Search on the device and on the server.
* A look modeled on iOS Mail: large titles, a bottom bar with filter, search and compose, message previews, a mailboxes menu. Dark theme, dynamic colors, three densities.

Not included yet: push notifications (mail arrives with the periodic sync, about every 15 minutes), rich text, PGP and S/MIME.

Principles:
* No telemetry, no analytics, no ads, no Google services. Your mail only travels between your device and your mail servers.
* Free software under the GPL-3.0-or-later license.
* English and Spanish.
